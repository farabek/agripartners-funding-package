## The short explanation

The Hissar Pilot models a controlled sheep-breeding project. The USD 50,000 is physical Pilot
Capital: it pays for breeding animals, a shelter, and operating liquidity. It is separate from
the USD 40,000 Development Round and from AgriPartners OÜ Corporate Setup.

## Where the USD 50,000 goes

| Initial use | Amount | What it enables |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 38 breeding ewes | USD 26,600 | Initial breeding flock assumed by the model |
| Sheep shelter | USD 18,000 | Physical shelter used by the project |
| Operating reserve | USD 5,400 | Initial operating liquidity and approved project costs |
| **Total** | **USD 50,000** | **Complete illustrative starting capital** |

These are model assumptions, not supplier quotations. Before real funding, animal count and
quality, prices, ownership, permitted use, invoices, contingency, and evidence requirements must
be approved in the project-specific budget and signed agreements.

## What the model demonstrates

| Measure | Illustrative value |
| --- | ---: |
| Production plan | 6 cycles of 6 months |
| Model duration | 36 months |
| Economic split | 60/40 model |
| Projected investor payout | USD 81,672 |
| Projected total ROI | 63.3% |
| Simple annualized ROI | 21.1% |

Simple annualized ROI divides the projected total ROI by the three-year duration. It is not APR
or IRR and does not reflect compounding or the timing of interim payments. Every value is
projected, not promised or guaranteed.

## Who does what

- **External Investor:** contracts with and funds AgriPartners OÜ; there is no direct transfer to
  the Uzbekistan operator or Farmer.
- **AgriPartners OÜ:** legal counterparty responsible for approved conversion, banking,
  accounting, reconciliation, governance, and investor reporting.
- **Approved Uzbekistan operator:** receives and returns fiat only under a separate written
  agreement and is responsible for operational delivery and evidence.
- **Farmer product role:** records flock activity, reports, and confirmations; it has no crypto
  wallet or required on-chain payment duty.
- **NEAR:** may support Estonia/investor-side audit records and automation but never replaces
  contracts, bank records, invoices, accounting, or settlement evidence.

## Project lifecycle

`Approval → funding → fiat disbursement → farmer confirmation → 6 production cycles → reports → reconciliation → settlement → closeout`

Capital must not be released merely because the model exists. Each transition requires its own
evidence and approval.

## Main risks

- animal health, mortality, fertility, birth outcomes, feed, weather, and biosecurity;
- purchase and sale prices, feed costs, demand, liquidity, currency movements, and delays;
- operator execution, misuse of funds, reporting quality, transport, site, and counterparty risk;
- legal, tax, banking, accounting, payments, and cross-border compliance risk.

The modeled Protection Reserve accumulates only after successful cycles. It is not fully funded
at the start, is not insurance, and cannot eliminate or guarantee compensation for loss.

## What must happen before a real pilot

1. Establish and prepare AgriPartners OÜ and approved banking/payment infrastructure.
2. Complete qualified legal, tax, accounting, compliance, and cross-border review.
3. Select and verify the operator, site, suppliers, flock, veterinary controls, and buyers.
4. Replace assumptions with quotations, due diligence, a project budget, and evidence rules.
5. Execute Investor, Operator, and other required agreements and disclosures.
6. Complete a Hissar-specific Operational Readiness review and record an explicit GO decision.
7. Confirm that the full Pilot Capital is available or legally committed before release.
