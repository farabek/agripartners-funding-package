**Status date:** 3 September 2026  
**Current public status:** no funder, investor, grant, accelerator, or strategic partner is publicly confirmed for any component.

## The short answer

AgriPartners currently has four separate financing components. The table below identifies the
types of funding source that may fit each component; it does not claim that any organization has
approved or committed money.

| Component | Target | Suitable funding-source categories | Current status |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Development Round | USD 40,000 | Technical/ecosystem grant, accelerator, venture studio, angel/pre-seed or strategic investor | **Seeking qualified source — not committed** |
| Corporate Setup | Initial target EUR 15,000–20,000 | Founder bridge, readiness/accelerator grant, angel/pre-seed or strategic investor, separately eligible part of a broader commitment | **Seeking qualified source — not committed** |
| Feedlot Pilot | USD 50,000 | Project or impact investor, agricultural strategic partner, family office, approved operator co-investment | **Seeking qualified source — not committed** |
| Hissar Sheep Pilot | USD 50,000 | Separate project or impact investor, agricultural strategic partner, family office, approved operator co-investment | **Seeking qualified source — not committed** |

One organization may finance more than one component, but each component must remain separately
identified in the agreement, budget, eligibility rules, accounts, permitted use, approvals,
reporting, and evidence.

## Why there is no organization name here

A researched prospect, submitted application, meeting, verbal expression of interest, or
conditional discussion is not confirmed funding. AgriPartners will name a financing party
publicly only when authoritative evidence and any required disclosure approval support that
statement.

The private HQ workspace holds named prospects, contacts, pipeline stages, next actions, and
evidence. Public documentation reports only a supportable status and suitable categories.

## Component rules

### 1. Development Round — USD 40,000

This is a product-only request for the defined Beta Candidate milestone. Non-dilutive technical
or ecosystem funding is preferred where it fits, but accelerator, venture-studio, angel,
pre-seed, strategic, or impact funding may also be considered. The approved scope must remain
unchanged and separately accounted for.

[See how the USD 40,000 budget works](BUDGET_EXPLAINED.md).

### 2. Corporate Setup — initial target EUR 15,000–20,000

This component prepares AgriPartners OÜ for agreements, accounting, KYB, banking/payments, legal
and regulatory review, privacy, and funding administration. It needs founder/unrestricted support
or funding that expressly permits these costs. Restricted Development funds cannot be used unless
the agreement separately authorizes them.

[See the Corporate Setup budget](CORPORATE_SETUP_BUDGET.md).

### 3. Feedlot Pilot — USD 50,000

This is project-specific physical Pilot Capital. A suitable source may be a project investor,
impact investor, agricultural partner, family office, or approved operator co-investment. The
commitment must have its own agreements, conditions, permitted use, evidence, and settlement
rules. Capital release requires Feedlot Operational Readiness and an explicit GO.

[See the Feedlot Pilot and allocation](FEEDLOT_PILOT_EXPLAINED.md).

### 4. Hissar Sheep Pilot — USD 50,000

Hissar requires its own project-specific source and evidence package. Feedlot funding or approval
does not finance or authorize Hissar. Capital release requires a Hissar-specific Operational
Readiness Review and explicit GO.

[See the Hissar Pilot and allocation](HISSAR_PILOT_EXPLAINED.md).

## Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Seeking / Research | Potential sources are being identified; no commitment exists |
| Qualified | Eligibility, amount, scope, timing, and route have been checked |
| Contacted / Submitted | A dated outreach or application exists; approval is not implied |
| In discussion | A response or meeting exists; funding is still not committed |
| Conditional | Written terms or an award notice exists but conditions remain |
| Committed | An executed agreement or authoritative commitment exists |
| Received | Funds are supported by bank/payment and accounting evidence |

## Planned sequence

Research and outreach may proceed in parallel. Deployment remains gated:

`Development + Corporate Setup → accepted product and corporate readiness → Operational Readiness → Feedlot GO → Hissar-specific GO`

Neither pilot may accept or deploy live capital until the applicable corporate, legal, financial,
operational, disclosure, and governance conditions are satisfied.

## Public update rule

This page must be updated when a component receives authoritative conditional, committed, or
received status. Until then, every component remains **not committed**. This page is explanatory
planning information, not an offer, solicitation, endorsement claim, or promise of funding.
