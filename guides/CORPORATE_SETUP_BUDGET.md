## The short explanation

The USD 40,000 Development Round pays only for the defined product milestone.
Creating and preparing AgriPartners OÜ is a separate Corporate Setup component.

There are three different cost levels:

1. register and maintain a basic Estonian OÜ;
2. prepare the company to contract for and receive development funding;
3. prepare for live investment and production financial operations.

They must not be treated as the same level of readiness.

## Level 1 — basic OÜ and first-year administration

The official Estonian e-Residency guidance currently presents these baseline
costs:

| Item | Published baseline | Explanation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| e-Residency application, if required | €150 | Digital identity application state fee |
| OÜ registration | €265 | State fee for online private limited company registration |
| Legal address and licensed contact person | €200–400/year | Required arrangement when applicable to a management board outside Estonia |
| Business account opening | €0–200 | Indicative range; provider approval is not guaranteed |
| Bookkeeping/accounting | from €50/month | Entry-level service pricing; activity and reporting increase the cost |
| Minimum share capital | from €0.01/shareholder | Company capital, not a service fee; it belongs to the company |
| **Calculated first-year baseline** | **€1,215–1,615** | Before translations, advice, enhanced due diligence, or additional administration |

For realistic planning, allow **€2,000–4,000** for a basic first year because
service packages, translations, certified documents, and onboarding complexity
may add cost.

## Level 2 — ready to receive the Development Round

Registration alone does not make the company ready to sign a funding agreement,
pass KYB, account for restricted development funds, or satisfy a funder's due
diligence. The following amounts are **internal planning allowances**, not
official tariffs or quotes:

| Component | Planning range | Expected output |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Formation and first-year administration | €2,000–4,000 | Registered OÜ, address/contact person, corporate records and administration |
| Founder and corporate documents | €1,500–4,000 | Founder/shareholder arrangements, authorities, resolutions and governance records |
| Legal and regulatory assessment | €3,000–8,000 | Written review of company, funding, product and cross-border structure |
| Contracts, privacy and data protection | €2,000–5,000 | Funding and service documents, privacy/terms review and relevant agreements |
| Accounting and tax setup | €1,200–3,000 | Chart of accounts, bookkeeping process, reporting and tax setup |
| Banking, payment and KYB preparation | €500–2,000 | Application materials, provider onboarding and account controls |
| Controlled contingency | €1,500–3,000 | Approved unexpected readiness work |
| **Full planning range** | **€11,700–29,000** | Depends on scope, providers and due-diligence requirements |

A practical initial planning target is **€15,000–20,000**, subject to written
professional scopes and quotations. It is not a promise that every item in the
full high-range scope can be completed within €20,000.

## Level 3 — live investment and financial operations

Before AgriPartners accepts real investor funds or operates production payment
flows, it may additionally require:

- regulatory classification of the investment model;
- securities, crowdfunding, payments and virtual-asset analysis;
- KYC, KYB, AML, sanctions and source-of-funds controls;
- Investor participation documents;
- Estonia–Uzbekistan Operator agreements;
- approved crypto-to-fiat arrangements, where applicable;
- fiat disbursement, segregation, treasury and reconciliation procedures;
- licensed partners, regulatory registrations, permissions, or licences if
  required by the final model.

Use **€20,000–50,000+** only as an early planning envelope. It excludes any
regulatory minimum capital, security deposit, licence cost, audit, or extensive
cross-border opinion. No final budget should be approved until qualified
Estonian counsel classifies the actual model in writing.

## Banking and payment boundary

Opening an account is not the same as production financial readiness. Every
bank, EMI, payment provider, or conversion provider applies its own eligibility,
country, industry, ownership, KYB and risk rules. e-Residency does not guarantee
account approval.

Under the AgriPartners operating boundary:

- approved investor or grant capital is received by AgriPartners OÜ;
- any approved crypto stops in Estonia and must be converted through approved
  infrastructure before Uzbekistan activity is financed;
- the Uzbekistan Operator receives and returns fiat only;
- bank, provider, accounting and reconciliation records remain authoritative.

Provider fees and transaction charges must be added only after provider
selection and written pricing. See the relevant provider's current pricing,
for example [Wise Business pricing](https://wise.com/gb/pricing/business), but
do not assume eligibility or approval from a published price page.

## What is included in each financing component

| Component | Purpose | Current planning amount |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Development Round | Build and verify the Beta Candidate product milestone | USD 40,000 |
| Corporate Setup | Create and prepare AgriPartners OÜ to receive development funding | Initial target €15,000–20,000 |
| Feedlot Pilot | Separately controlled physical pilot capital | USD 50,000 |
| Hissar Sheep Pilot | Separately controlled physical pilot capital | USD 50,000 |
| Live regulated infrastructure | Defined only after legal classification | Early envelope €20,000–50,000+ |

Currency differences are intentional. The official Development and Pilot
objectives are recorded in USD; Estonian setup and professional costs are
planned in EUR. Conversion must use the actual rate and agreement terms when a
funding proposal is prepared.
