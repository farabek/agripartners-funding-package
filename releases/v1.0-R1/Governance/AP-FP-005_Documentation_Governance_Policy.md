# Documentation Governance Policy

## 1. Authority hierarchy

Funding Strategy v1 governs funding paths, component separation and financial architecture. This Funding Package governs the Development Round’s operational scope, Work Packages, Deliverables, budget, Milestones, evidence and acceptance. Product, Business, Engineering and release authorities continue to govern their respective domains.

## 2. Canonical sources

Each Section file is a canonical source. Sections 1, 3, 5 and 13 are Reissued Canonical Sources. The other Section files are Preserved Approved Sources. The Master Markdown is an assembly of those Section sources. DOCX and PDF are publication renderings; where rendering differs from source text, the Section Markdown and Freeze Registry control.

## 3. Freeze and change control

Publication Edition 1.0 is frozen. Editorial corrections that do not change meaning require a recorded patch revision. Any change to scope, budget, milestone logic, financial boundary, acceptance standard or factual product claim requires explicit approval, Revision History entry, updated Freeze Registry and regenerated outputs.

## 4. Derived adaptations

Grant, accelerator, investor and partner adaptations must comply with Section 21. They may change format and emphasis but may not modify the authoritative core.

## 5. Evidence and factual integrity

Current capability, proposed capability and post-acceptance capability must remain distinct. Repository activity, expenditure, Testnet records or presentation materials do not independently establish completion, legal effect, fiat clearing, production settlement or Operational Readiness.

## 6. Terminology

Appendix A is the controlled vocabulary. Defined governance objects use consistent names and capitalization. “Production ready”, “investment ready”, “audited”, “mainnet ready”, “live platform”, “regulated platform” and equivalent claims are prohibited unless separately established by competent evidence and authority.

## 7. Publication integrity

Every release must contain the Master Markdown, DOCX, PDF, individual Sections, Appendices, governance instruments, QA reports, manifest and SHA-256 checksum file. A hash mismatch invalidates the affected artifact’s frozen status.
